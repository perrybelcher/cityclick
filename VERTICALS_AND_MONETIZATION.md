## 2. Multi-Tiered Revenue Stack

CityClick leverages three complementary revenue engines to maximize revenue per thousand visitors (RPM):

```
                        CityClick Visitor
                                │
               ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
               ▼                                 ▼
         Phone Callers                      Web Submissions
         (~65% of traffic)                  (~35% of traffic)
               │                                 │
               ▼                                 ▼
      [ Dynamic Pay-Per-Call ]           [ Dual-Lead Dispatch ]
      - $50 to $185 per call             - $35 to $120 per lead
               │                                 │
               └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                                │
                                ▼
              [ Recurring Pro Directory Subscriptions ]
              - $99/mo "CityClick Certified" Listing
              - $297/mo "Exclusive City Spotlight"
```

### 1. Dynamic Pay-Per-Call (PPC) Telephony
* **Immediate Cash Generation:** No complex sales cycle required initially. Traffic is routed directly to pre-established national pay-per-call buyer networks (e.g., Ringba marketplace, Service Direct, Aragon Advertising) with guaranteed payouts per qualified 90-second call.
* **Margin Expansion (Local Contractor Direct):** Once volume in a target metro stabilizes (e.g. Dallas Roofing), direct outreach transitions the territory from national broker payout ($95/call) to direct contractor billing ($150/call), yielding a 58% instant margin lift.

### 2. Dual-Dispatch Web & SMS Leads
* Embedded dynamic estimate calculators and emergency response request forms capture users who prefer digital interaction.
* Instant SMS dispatch to enrolled local contractors with a 1-tap call-back link.
* Unclaimed leads dynamically routed to regional lead aggregation partners.

### 3. "CityClick Certified" Directory Subscriptions
* Local contractors pay a recurring monthly fee ($99–$297/mo) for:
  - Top organic badge placement on their municipal category page.
  - Profile customization (portfolio photos, verified license details, customer testimonials).
  - Dofollow authority backlink from high-authority exact-match domain (`cityclick.com`).
  - Zero competitor advertising displayed on their dedicated profile view.
